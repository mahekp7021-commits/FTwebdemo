Platform choice

A trading platform is the interface used to view prices, analyse charts, place orders and manage positions. Different platforms can offer different layouts, indicators, order types and automation options.

Desktop, browser and mobile

Desktop applications may suit detailed charting and multi-window workflows. Browser-based platforms can reduce installation needs, while mobile apps support monitoring and account management while away from a computer. Availability depends on the platform and account.

Check before connecting

Use the correct server or account credentials, confirm the supported instruments and read platform-specific documentation. Demo environments and live accounts may have different functionality or data conditions.
