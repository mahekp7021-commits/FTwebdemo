Understand the programme

Review the current agreement, qualifying activity, attribution rules, commission calculation, payment schedule and any restrictions on promotion before joining.

Transparent reporting

Partners should understand how referrals are tracked, how eligible activity is calculated and how adjustments or disputes are handled. Confirm which reporting tools are available.

Responsible promotion

Do not promise profits, guaranteed returns or risk-free trading. Ensure marketing statements are accurate and consistent with applicable legal and partner terms.
