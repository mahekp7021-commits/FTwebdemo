How CFDs work

A contract for difference provides exposure to the price movement of an underlying market without generally owning that underlying asset. The result depends on the price difference and the applicable costs.

Markets to explore

Market categories can include currencies, indices, shares, commodities and digital assets where available. Each product has its own specifications, trading hours and risks.

Before placing a trade

Review spread, any commission, margin, leverage, overnight financing, order size and the risk disclosure for the exact instrument.
