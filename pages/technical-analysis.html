Explore technical analysis tools

Technical analysis uses price charts, indicators and market structure to organise observations about past and current price behaviour.

Important: Market information and tools are educational in nature. They do not guarantee results or remove the risks of trading leveraged products.

Charts and trends

Traders may review trend direction, support and resistance areas, volatility and price patterns. Interpretations can differ and may fail when market conditions change.

Indicators are not guarantees

Indicators are based on historical or current market data. Signals can be late, conflicting or misleading, especially during sharp moves or low liquidity.

Build a repeatable process

Define the timeframe, entry conditions, exit rules and risk limit in advance. Test methods carefully and avoid relying on a single indicator.
