Payment · Withdrawal

Withdraw funds securely

Submit withdrawal requests through the verified account portal and review the applicable verification steps, payment rules and processing conditions.

Contact supportPayment overview

Submit a withdrawal request

Use the official account portal to submit a request and follow the current verification steps. Confirm the available balance, any open-position margin requirements and the payment method rules before submitting.

Verification and destination

The account provider may need to verify identity, payment ownership or source-of-funds information. Withdrawals may be returned or delayed if the destination details are incomplete, incorrect or not eligible under the applicable payment rules.

Processing time and fees

Processing time can vary with internal reviews, banking networks, weekends, holidays and the receiving provider. Check the current terms for any minimum or maximum amounts, conversion costs or withdrawal charges before confirming.

Track and protect your request

Keep the request reference and any confirmation message. Never give a password or OTP to a person who says they can release a withdrawal for a fee. If a request is unfamiliar, contact official support immediately.
