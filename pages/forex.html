FOREX KNOWLEDGE

Understand the market before
you trade it.

Strong trading starts with understanding the market, its instruments and the factors that can influence currency prices.

What moves currency prices?

Interest-rate expectations, economic releases, inflation, employment data, geopolitical developments and changes in market sentiment can all influence currency prices.

Why risk management matters

Forex trading involves significant risk. Position sizing, stop-loss planning, diversification and a clear understanding of leverage should form part of any responsible trading approach.

Choosing the right platform

Select a platform that matches your preferred devices, analytical tools and trading workflow.

Build a disciplined process

A consistent trading plan helps define objectives, evaluate market conditions and manage decisions with greater discipline.
