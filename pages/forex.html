01 / THE MARKET

One market.
Many currency relationships.

Forex (foreign exchange) is the market where one currency is exchanged for another. A pair shows how much of the quote currency is needed to buy one unit of the base currency.

01
↔

Major pairs

Pairs built around heavily followed currencies, commonly including the US dollar, euro, Japanese yen and British pound.

03 / WHAT MOVES FX?

Prices move.
Understand why.

No single factor explains every currency movement. Traders commonly monitor a combination of economic data, monetary policy and changes in global risk sentiment.
