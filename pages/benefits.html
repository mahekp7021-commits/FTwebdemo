What makes the experience work.

These benefits describe the website and product experience rather than making unsupported claims about execution, pricing or regulation.

01 · Broad market discovery

Dedicated market pages help traders explore Forex, Commodities, Indices, Shares CFDs and Cryptocurrency in an organised way.

02 · Market information on site

Live market and calendar tools keep useful market information within the same branded experience.

03 · Platform choice made simple

MT4, MT5 and WebTrader each have their own page so traders can understand the workflow before moving to account access.

04 · Clear account hierarchy

Six account types are grouped into Standard, Premium and Professional families for easier comparison.

05 · Consistent responsive experience

The navigation and core workflows are designed to remain usable on smaller screens as well as desktop.

06 · Connected calls to action

Buttons lead to relevant destinations such as account opening, platform pages, market pages and company information instead of dead-end sections.
