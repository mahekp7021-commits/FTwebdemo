What are commodity markets?

Commodities include energy products, precious metals and agricultural products. Many retail trading products provide price exposure through derivatives rather than ownership or delivery of the physical commodity.

What can move prices?

Supply disruptions, production decisions, inventory data, seasonal patterns, currency movements and changes in global demand can all influence prices. These factors can change quickly and may increase volatility.

Build a risk-aware approach

Understand the specific product, trading hours, contract specifications, financing costs and margin rules before placing an order. Consider how gaps and fast markets can affect stop orders and available account equity.
