Payment · Deposit

Deposit funds securely

Review available funding routes, processing considerations and the checks to complete before adding funds to an FXDemo account.

Contact supportPayment overview
Payment safety: Always use the payment instructions displayed in your verified account portal. Methods, processing times, fees and eligibility may vary by region and account. Never share passwords or one-time codes.

Before making a deposit

Sign in through the official account portal and check the funding methods actually enabled for your account and region. The beneficiary details, payment references, minimums and accepted currencies may differ by method.

Bank transfer, QR and UPI

The GOCOIIN reference menu describes bank transfer, QR and UPI details. Availability can depend on country, account status and payment provider. Only use the exact payment instructions shown inside the verified account portal; never send funds to details received from an unverified person.

Processing and account credit

Processing time may depend on the payment network, bank cut-off times, weekends, public holidays, compliance checks and whether the payment reference is correct. Keep your transaction receipt until the funds appear in your account.

Important safety checks

Never share passwords, OTPs or full card details with anyone claiming to arrange a deposit. Do not pay an unexpected third party or use a wallet/account name that does not match the official instructions. Contact support through the official channel if details appear inconsistent.
