Trading · Getting started

Understand the account-opening journey

A clear process starts with reviewing product information, checking eligibility, completing required verification and understanding the platform before funding an account.

Explore account optionsContact
Risk awareness: Trading leveraged products involves risk and may not be suitable for everyone. You can lose some or all of your invested capital. Review the applicable product terms, costs and risk disclosures before making a decision.

1. Review the information

Read the account terms, risk disclosures, product specifications, fees, supported markets and eligibility rules. Make sure the service is available in your jurisdiction.

2. Complete the application

Provide accurate information through the official account-opening process. Depending on the applicable requirements, identity, address, suitability or other verification checks may be required.

3. Learn the platform and manage risk

Before placing a live trade, understand order types, margin, position sizing, stop orders and how to monitor open positions. A demo environment, if available, can help familiarise you with platform controls but does not remove live-market risk.
