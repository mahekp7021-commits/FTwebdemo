Understand spreads, leverage and margin

Review the key trading conditions that affect the cost and risk of a position before you place an order.

Open AccountContact

Spreads and commissions

The spread is the difference between bid and ask prices. GOCOIIN account tiers may show different headline spreads, but actual quotes can vary by instrument, liquidity, market volatility and account terms. Check whether a commission also applies.

Leverage and margin

Leverage lets a trader control market exposure larger than the margin set aside. It can magnify both gains and losses. The supplied GOCOIIN reference lists leverage up to 1:400; confirm the limit applicable to your account, instrument and jurisdiction.

Execution and overnight costs

Execution may be affected by liquidity, volatility, connectivity and order type. Positions held overnight may incur financing adjustments. Read the current instrument specification for trading hours, margin rules and charges.
